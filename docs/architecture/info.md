# Info

This document covers Informace: the crisis contact, its two views with the message form, the notifications readers see, the quick links and the toast Informace shows.

## Informace

`InfoModule` (`/informace`, tab `Informace`, `templates/info.twig`) is the crisis contact, then `Notifikace` — the message log as readers see it: every visible message, newest first, under Prague day headings (`Push\NotificationList::groups()`, `Čt 20. 5.`, keyed for the morph), each card left-aligned with its full body and a relative time (`NotificationList::relative()`: under a minute `právě teď`, under 60 minutes always `před N min`, even across midnight; the same Prague day `před N h`; the day before `včera H:i`; older a date), `Zatím žádné notifikace.` when there is none — then `Rychlé odkazy` (the handbook and `content/links.php`). Who sees a programme's message here is in `Info\InfoScreen`'s docblock: everybody reads it in that programme's sheet on `/programy`. Every relative time carries its instant in `data-sent-at`, here, on Domů and in a programme's sheet, and `www/js/ui/reltime.js` redraws it from the device clock with the same bands, so a copy read later or offline still says how long ago it is. There is no archive or detail screen. Because of that `info` requires `push`: `EventConfig::load()` refuses an event that enables one without the other, and every shipped event carries both.

The cards are `Push\Notification`s, grouped by day into `NotificationGroup`s (`key` for the morph, `label` the heading), and `Info\InfoScreen` renders them with the crisis contact, the quick links, a waiting toast, the view the address names and, with `inbox`, the message form's `Inbox\View\InboxPage` as the `Info\View\InfoPage`. `Info\ShowAction` calls it for both of the screen's addresses, `Inbox\SendAction` for a refused send.

## Two views: Informace and Zprávy

An event with `inbox` (obrok19 and obrok27) gives the screen a second view, the message form, on a second address: `/<slug>/informace` paints the Informace view, `/<slug>/informace/zpravy` the Zprávy view, switched by a floating pill `Informace | Zprávy` exactly like Místa's `Mapa | Plán`. Both panes are always in the markup, the pills are links (without JavaScript, a full page load; with it, `www/js/inbox/views.js` switches in place and pushes the address), and the Informace tab lists the second address as its alias, so a tab tap comes back to the view the screen was left on, while a notification opens `/informace`. There is no card to the form any more: the pill is the only way in. An event without `inbox` has one view, no pills, and the markup it always had. The markup, the routes, the script and the form are in [inbox.md](inbox.md).

## The toast

Informace takes a waiting server toast (`Toast\Toasts::take()`) on a full page and renders it on top, as Profil and Program do, and marks the response (`X-Toast: 1`) so the worker does not store it. The one toast that lands here is the sent message's, `Zpráva odeslána, díky!`, after the redirect to the Zprávy view, `/<slug>/informace/zpravy#odeslano` ([inbox.md](inbox.md)); it sits above both views. `InfoScreen` takes the toast before it reads the participant, because that read clears a waiting toast when it finds the session gone. Informace leaves no `gone()` toast of its own: Profil, not Informace, explains a session that kissj no longer knows.

## Quick links and the crisis contact

`content/links.php` is Informace's `Rychlé odkazy`: a `<ul class="link-list">` of `.link-card`s (the handbook first and tonal, a `highlight` entry tonal too, an optional `note` as a second line, an outward mark on absolute URLs, which open in a new tab — `target="_blank" rel="noopener"`, as do Domů's external `highlight` CTAs and its footer link), while Domů shows only the `highlight` ones as `.highlight` CTAs. `content/emergency.php` (`contacts`: `label`, `phone`, optional `note`; `info`: lines) is Informace's crisis contact (`Krizový kontakt akce`, each contact with a `Zavolat` button that is a `tel:` link, spaces stripped, so it dials from the worker's offline copy). The list is the partial `templates/_emergency_contacts.twig` (`contacts`), which the Zprávy view includes too, above its form ([inbox.md](inbox.md)); its rules stay in `www/css/info.css`, linked on every page; an event without the file shows none; obrok27 carries the real crisis line `+420 704 444 015`; navigamus27 and korbo26 have published none, so theirs is the general `112` until they do. Quick links (`content/links.php`): obrok19, obrok27, navigamus27 (its site and `info@navigamus.cz`) and korbo26 (its site, FAQ and Instagram).

`EventConfig::links()` reads `links.php` as a list of `Event\Link` (`label`, `href`, `icon`, `suffix`, `highlight`, `note`), `[]` without the file, and `emergency()` reads `emergency.php` as an `Event\Emergency`, null without the file.
